We design cloud infrastructure that's built for reality — not just diagrams. Production-grade architectures across AWS, GCP, Azure, and bare metal that scale with your business.

##### **What We Design**

- **Multi-cloud & hybrid architectures** — workloads placed where they make technical and financial sense
- **Bare metal Kubernetes** — for when cloud costs don't justify the workload, or compliance requires on-prem
- **Network architecture** — VPCs, peering, transit gateways, and CNI selection (Cilium, Calico) for K8s networking
- **Storage solutions** — Ceph, Longhorn, and cloud-native storage for stateful workloads
- **Hybrid workloads** — KubeVirt for running VMs on Kubernetes, enabling gradual migration from traditional infrastructure without forklift rewrites
- **High availability patterns** — multi-AZ, multi-region, active-active and active-passive failover
- **Disaster recovery** — RTO/RPO planning, backup strategies, automated failover testing

##### **Our Process**

1. **Discovery** — understand your workloads, traffic patterns, compliance requirements, and budget
2. **Architecture** — design the target state with detailed diagrams, component selection, and cost estimates
3. **Implementation** — build it out with IaC (Terraform), GitOps, and proper CI/CD from day one
4. **Validation** — load testing, chaos engineering, failover drills

##### **Why It Matters**

- Infrastructure decisions made early compound — good architecture saves months of rework
- Right-sized from the start: no over-provisioning, no surprise bills
- Security and compliance baked into the foundation, not patched in later
- A clear roadmap your team can execute against

We don't just draw architecture diagrams. We build the infrastructure and operate it alongside you.
