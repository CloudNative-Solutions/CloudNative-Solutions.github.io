Stop building infrastructure tickets. We design and build Internal Developer Platforms that let your engineers ship code without waiting on ops.

##### What We Build

- **Developer Portals** — Backstage-based service catalogs with golden paths, templates, and self-service infrastructure provisioning.
- **GitOps Everything** — ArgoCD with app-of-apps patterns, ApplicationSets for multi-cluster, Kargo for promotion pipelines across environments.
- **Automated Workflows** — Argo Events for event-driven automation, Argo Workflows for CI/CD pipelines, build systems with Gitea and Harbor.
- **Progressive Delivery** — Argo Rollouts for canary and blue-green deployments. Automated rollback on metric degradation.
- **Self-Service Infrastructure** — Developers get namespaces, databases, secrets, and monitoring automatically. No tickets, no waiting.

##### Our Stack

Backstage · ArgoCD · Kargo · Argo Workflows · Argo Events · Argo Rollouts · Gitea · Harbor · Helm · Kustomize

##### Why Us

Our own platform manages 84 applications across 15 categories with zero manual deployments. Every change goes through GitOps — from infrastructure to application config. We practice what we build.
